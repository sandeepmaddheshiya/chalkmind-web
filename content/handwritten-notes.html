How students use it

Write, understand, then test yourself

Most notes apps are good at storing notes. Chalkmind is built to help you learn from them, because rereading notes is the least effective way to revise.

1

Write in class

Lined pages for essays, grid pages for maths and science, an endless canvas for mind maps. Rough circles, boxes and arrows snap into neat shapes.

2

Fill the gaps

Circle anything you didn't follow and tap Explain. Highlight unfamiliar words in a PDF to see what they mean in that sentence.

3

Revise actively

Select a page and ask for flashcards, practice questions or a quiz, and answer them before you look back at your notes.

Revision tools from your own handwriting

Because the assistant reads the page you select, the questions are about your notes, not a generic textbook version of the topic.

  • Flashcards: short question-and-answer cards from the page
  • Practice questions: five questions like yours, getting harder
  • Quiz: five multiple-choice questions with answers
  • Revision notes: a tidy summary of a messy page
  • Summarise: the main points in a few lines
  • Mind map: an editable map of a topic to rearrange and extend

Especially good for maths and science

  • End a problem with “=” to see the answer in your handwriting
  • Check your working for the first mistake before an exam
  • Plot equations as graphs next to your notes
  • Balance chemical equations and check physics calculations
  • Keep a “mistakes I make” page with corrections explained
Lecture slides and handouts

Notes around the slides, not copies of them

Copying slides word for word wastes the lesson. Import the slides as a PDF before class and write only what the teacher adds: explanations, examples, and what they say will be on the test.

Fast to open

Pages load as you reach them, so even a long slide deck or textbook chapter opens quickly.

Look words up in place

Highlight a term for its meaning in context, then add the explanation to the page as a note.

Export for revision

Export the annotated pages as a PDF to print, share with a study group, or back up.

What Chalkmind doesn't do (yet)

We'd rather you choose the right app than be disappointed later:

  • It doesn't convert handwriting into typed text or search inside handwriting.
  • Notes aren't synced between devices; export PDFs for backups.
  • It's Android only, and works best on a tablet with a stylus.
  • AI revision tools need a free sign-in and have a daily limit on the free plan.
