How it works

Read, mark up and understand in one place

No switching to a dictionary app or a search engine in the middle of revision.

1

Import

Choose Import PDF on the home screen, or add a PDF to any board. Each page becomes a page you can write on.

2

Highlight

Use the highlighter over a word, a phrase or a paragraph, just as you would on paper.

3

Understand

Tap Meaning for the word in context, or Explain for a plain-language explanation. Add it to the page as a note.

For students

  • Make sense of dense textbook paragraphs without leaving the page
  • Solve worksheet questions in the margin, with Check my work for maths
  • Turn a chapter into flashcards or practice questions for revision
  • Keep annotated pages as a PDF for later

For teachers

  • Put a worksheet on the projector and work through it live
  • Explain vocabulary as it comes up, and keep the explanation on the page
  • Export the annotated worksheet to share with the class
  • Generate a quiz from the same chapter in a few seconds
Example

Meaning versus Explain

Highlight “neutralisation” in a chemistry chapter:

Meaning

The reaction of an acid with a base that cancels out both, producing a salt and water.

Highlight the whole paragraph about it and tap Explain:

Explain

This paragraph says that acids and bases react to cancel each other out. The products are always a salt and water; for example, hydrochloric acid and sodium hydroxide make sodium chloride (table salt) and water. The reaction gives off heat, which is why the temperature of the mixture rises.

Meaning is quick, for when a single word stops you. Explain is for paragraphs you've read twice and still don't follow.

Ways to use PDFs in Chalkmind

  • Lecture slides: write the teacher's explanations around each slide instead of copying the slide.
  • Worksheets and past papers: answer directly on the page, then check maths working for mistakes.
  • Textbook chapters: highlight key terms, look up the hard ones, and add short notes in the margin.
  • Reading in a second language: look up words in context without switching to a translator.
  • Teaching: project a worksheet and annotate it live with the class.

Tips for good results

  • Highlight the word itself for Meaning, and a full sentence or paragraph for Explain.
  • On scanned pages, highlight cleanly over the printed text so the area is easy to read.
  • Add useful explanations to the page as notes; they'll be there when you revise.
  • Export the annotated PDF when you finish a chapter, as a backup and a revision copy.

Limits

PDF pages are a background you write on; you can't edit the PDF's own text. Look-ups use the AI, so they need an internet connection and count towards the daily free AI answers. Very large files take longer on older devices.
