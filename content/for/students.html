A study session with Chalkmind

Maths homework, 30 minutes
  1. Import the worksheet PDF (or write the questions on a grid page).
  2. Do each question yourself, one step per line.
  3. Stuck for more than a few minutes? Ask for a hint, not the answer.
  4. Finished a question? Select it and tap Check my work.
  5. Got one wrong? Copy the corrected step onto a “mistakes I make” page.
Revision for a test
  1. Open your notes for the topic.
  2. Select a page and ask for a quiz or flashcards.
  3. Answer from memory first, then check against your notes.
  4. Ask for practice questions on anything you got wrong.
  5. Make a mind map of the whole topic and fill in the gaps.

Use it in a way your teacher would be happy with

AI can make homework feel finished without you learning anything, and that shows up in tests. Ask for hints before answers, always do your own working, and make sure you can explain every step you hand in. Follow your school's rules about which tools are allowed for which work.
