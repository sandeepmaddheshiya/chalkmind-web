What changed

The 2026 Microsoft Whiteboard changes, in plain terms

Based on Microsoft's Message Center notices (dates as published; Microsoft adjusted the timeline more than once, so check the latest notice for your account):

DateWhat happened
September 25, 2026Personal Microsoft accounts could no longer create or edit whiteboards.
October 16, 2026Standalone Whiteboard apps on Windows, iOS and Android no longer supported, for both personal and work or school accounts. Legacy whiteboards that weren't migrated or exported were scheduled for permanent deletion.
Going forwardWhiteboard continues in Microsoft Teams and on the web for work and school accounts, with boards stored in OneDrive.

Sources: MC1441775 and MC1469557 (Microsoft 365 Message Center).

Before you switch: save your boards

  1. If you still have access, open each whiteboard you need and export it as an image or PDF.
  2. Save exports somewhere permanent (OneDrive, Google Drive or your computer), organised by class or topic.
  3. School accounts: ask your IT team whether boards were migrated to OneDrive, and whether Whiteboard in Teams covers what you need.
  4. In a new app, import the exported PDFs and keep teaching from them. In Chalkmind you can write directly on top.

Chalkmind is a good replacement if you…

  • Used Whiteboard on a personal account and can no longer edit boards
  • Teach maths or science from an Android tablet on a projector or call
  • Want answers, steps and explanations right on the board
  • Work from PDF worksheets and past papers

Look elsewhere if you…

  • Need students drawing on one shared board at the same time
  • Your school runs everything through Teams (Whiteboard still works there)
  • Teach mostly from Windows or an iPad
