  • You use an iPhone or iPad

Camera solvers vs. a whiteboard: the real difference

A camera solver starts with a finished question and gives you a finished answer. That's fast, but your own thinking isn't part of it. Chalkmind starts with your working: you write the problem and your steps, and it can solve, give a hint, or check exactly where your working went wrong.

That difference matters most when you're learning a topic for a test. Seeing a correct solution is useful; finding out which of your steps was wrong, and why, is what stops you making the same mistake in the exam.

Example: same question, two approaches

The question

Solve 2(x + 3) = 4x − 2. A student writes 2x + 3 = 4x − 2, then gets x = 2.5.

  • With a camera solver, you photograph the question and see the correct answer, x = 4, with steps. You'd still need to compare your working yourself to find the slip.
  • With Check my work, you select your own working and are told the first mistake is on line 1: the 2 must multiply both terms, so it's 2x + 6. Then 2x + 6 = 4x − 2 gives x = 4.

Switching from a camera app

  • Do homework on a tablet instead of paper, or import the worksheet as a PDF and write on it.
  • Use Hint before asking for the full solution.
  • Use Check my work before handing anything in.
  • For textbook questions on paper, attach a photo in the AI Assistant and ask for an explanation.
