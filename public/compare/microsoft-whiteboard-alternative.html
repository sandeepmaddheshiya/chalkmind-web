Looking for a Microsoft Whiteboard alternative? Here's what changed

In 2026 Microsoft retired the standalone Whiteboard apps for Windows, iOS and Android, and personal accounts lost the ability to create and edit boards. If you taught with it, here's how to keep your work and what to use instead.

What changed

The 2026 Microsoft Whiteboard changes, in plain terms

Based on Microsoft's Message Center notices (dates as published; Microsoft adjusted the timeline more than once, so check the latest notice for your account):

DateWhat happened
September 25, 2026Personal Microsoft accounts could no longer create or edit whiteboards.
October 16, 2026Standalone Whiteboard apps on Windows, iOS and Android no longer supported, for both personal and work or school accounts. Legacy whiteboards that weren't migrated or exported were scheduled for permanent deletion.
Going forwardWhiteboard continues in Microsoft Teams and on the web for work and school accounts, with boards stored in OneDrive.

Sources: MC1441775 and MC1469557 (Microsoft 365 Message Center).

Before you switch: save your boards

  1. If you still have access, open each whiteboard you need and export it as an image or PDF.
  2. Save exports somewhere permanent (OneDrive, Google Drive or your computer), organised by class or topic.
  3. School accounts: ask your IT team whether boards were migrated to OneDrive, and whether Whiteboard in Teams covers what you need.
  4. In a new app, import the exported PDFs and keep teaching from them. In Chalkmind you can write directly on top.

Questions

Is Microsoft Whiteboard shutting down?

Not completely. Microsoft ended support for the standalone apps on Windows, iOS and Android from October 16, 2026, and personal accounts lost the ability to create or edit boards in September 2026. Whiteboard continues inside Microsoft Teams and on the web for work and school accounts.

Can I still open my old whiteboards?

Legacy whiteboards that weren't migrated or exported were scheduled to be permanently deleted on October 16, 2026. If you still have access, export anything you need as soon as possible; check Microsoft's current guidance for your account type.

What's the best free alternative on Android?

On a Samsung Galaxy tablet, Samsung Notes covers plain writing for free. For teaching maths and science, Chalkmind's whiteboard is free, with AI help included up to a daily limit.

Can students collaborate on a Chalkmind board like in Whiteboard?

Not yet. If live collaboration on one shared canvas is essential, a collaborative tool such as Miro, or Whiteboard in Teams for school accounts, is a better fit.

Does Chalkmind need a Microsoft or Google account?

No account is needed for the whiteboard itself. AI features need a free sign-in with Google.
