What it can do

A normal board, until you need help

Pen, highlighter, eraser, shapes and pages feel like any good whiteboard. The difference is what happens when you select something.

Solve

End a problem with “=”. The answer is written in your handwriting, with steps you can place on the board.

Explain & hint

Circle a step, a diagram or a paragraph and ask why. Get a hint instead of an answer when you want to keep thinking.

Check my work

Find the first mistake in handwritten working, with a clear explanation of the fix.

Quiz & revise

Turn a page of notes into practice questions, a quiz, flashcards or revision notes.

Diagrams

Mind maps, flowcharts and concept maps drawn as editable shapes on the board.

PDFs

Import a worksheet or chapter, write on top, and highlight any word to see what it means.

Ink that keeps up with you

The board is built for writing, not for menus. Strokes appear instantly, rough shapes snap into neat ones when you want them to, and everything saves automatically.

  • Pages or an endless canvas; plain, ruled, grid and dark chalkboard backgrounds
  • Erase whole strokes or just the part you touch; undo per page
  • Laser pointer for teaching on a projector or video call
  • Export any page as a PDF or image to share

Private by design

  • Boards and PDFs are saved on your device, not uploaded
  • The AI only sees what you select or send
  • No ads, and no tracking SDKs in the app
  • Drawing works offline

What “AI whiteboard” means here

Lots of whiteboard apps have added AI that generates sticky notes or summaries of typed text. Chalkmind's AI works on handwriting: it reads the strokes you select, including equations, diagrams and labels, and responds on the same board. In practice that means:

  • Selection-aware: it answers about what you circled, not a generic version of the topic.
  • Answers on the board: solutions appear in handwriting that matches yours; diagrams arrive as editable shapes.
  • Pedagogy built in: Hint and Check my work are designed to keep the learner thinking.
  • Opt-in: nothing is sent unless you ask; the board works fully without AI.

Questions

What is an AI whiteboard?

A digital whiteboard that can understand its own contents. In Chalkmind you write normally, and the AI can solve, explain, summarise or quiz you on whatever you select.

Which devices does Chalkmind run on?

Android phones and tablets with Android 7.0 or later. It works best on a tablet with a stylus, such as a Samsung Galaxy Tab with an S Pen.

Can I use it without AI?

Yes. Pens, highlighters, shapes, pages, PDFs, export and the on-device maths solver work without an account or internet connection.

Can several people draw on the same board at once?

Not yet. Chalkmind is a personal and teaching whiteboard: one person writes, and you can share the screen in a video call or on a projector.

Is there an iPhone or Windows version?

Not at the moment. Chalkmind is Android only for now.

Which AI model does Chalkmind use?

Chalkmind uses a vision-capable large language model through its own server, which keeps API keys out of the app and applies fair-use limits. The model may change as better ones become available.
