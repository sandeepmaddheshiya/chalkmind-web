Why tutors like a pen-first board

  • Writing maths with a stylus is faster and clearer than typing equations or drawing with a mouse.
  • Answers appear in handwriting that matches yours, so the screen doesn't jump between fonts.
  • Hints let you guide the student without giving the answer away when they're nearly there.
  • Everything is saved on your device, ready to reuse with the next student.

Setup

What you need to tutor from an Android tablet

Tablet and stylus

A 10-inch or larger Android tablet with a stylus gives you room to write and clear handwriting on the student's screen.

A video-call app

Google Meet or Zoom on the same tablet. Share your screen, then switch to Chalkmind.

A second device (optional)

Join the call on a laptop or phone too, so you can see the student's face while the tablet shares the board.

Tutoring techniques that work well on a tablet

  • Let the student lead. Ask them to talk you through each step while you write it, then pause on the step where they hesitate.
  • Hint, don't tell. When a student is close, use a hint to point at the next step rather than solving it. They remember what they work out.
  • Collect their mistakes. Keep one page per student called “mistakes to watch”. Review it at the start of each session.
  • Parallel questions. After a worked example, generate a similar practice question for the student to solve while you watch.
  • End with a summary page. Two or three lines on what was covered and what to practise, exported with the session notes.

Using homework the student sends you

Students often send a photo of a worksheet or their attempt before a session. Import worksheets that arrive as PDFs and write directly on them during the call. For a photo of their working, attach it in the AI Assistant and ask it to check the work: you'll see where it first goes wrong before the session starts, so you can plan the lesson around the real misunderstanding.

Keeping things organised

Create one board per student, with a new page for each session. Boards are saved on your device automatically, so last week's work is there when the student says “we did this before”. Export a PDF after each session for the student or their parents.

What it isn't

Chalkmind isn't a shared board: the student watches your screen rather than writing on it. If you need students to draw on the same canvas at the same time, pair your video call with a collaborative whiteboard for those moments, and use Chalkmind for explaining.

Questions

Can my student write on my board?

Not at the moment. You write and share your screen. The student can send you a photo of their working, and you can attach it in the AI Assistant to explain it or check it.

Which video apps does it work with?

Any app that can share your Android screen, such as Google Meet or Zoom.

Can I prepare sessions in advance?

Yes. Prepare pages, import the student's worksheet as a PDF and have the assistant draft practice questions before the call.

How do I give students notes afterwards?

Export the board as a PDF or images and send it through your usual channel.

Can I use it for subjects other than maths?

Yes. The whiteboard, PDFs and the assistant's explanations, quizzes and mind maps work for any subject. Solving and checking are strongest in maths, physics and chemistry.

Does the student need to install anything?

No. They watch your shared screen in the video call and receive the exported PDF afterwards.
