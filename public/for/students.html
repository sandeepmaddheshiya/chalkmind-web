A study session with Chalkmind

Maths homework, 30 minutes
  1. Import the worksheet PDF (or write the questions on a grid page).
  2. Do each question yourself, one step per line.
  3. Stuck for more than a few minutes? Ask for a hint, not the answer.
  4. Finished a question? Select it and tap Check my work.
  5. Got one wrong? Copy the corrected step onto a “mistakes I make” page.
Revision for a test
  1. Open your notes for the topic.
  2. Select a page and ask for a quiz or flashcards.
  3. Answer from memory first, then check against your notes.
  4. Ask for practice questions on anything you got wrong.
  5. Make a mind map of the whole topic and fill in the gaps.

Questions

Is it cheating?

Not if you use it to learn. Ask for a hint before an answer, check your own working, and make sure you can explain each step. Follow your school's rules on what help is allowed.

Is it free for students?

The whiteboard, PDFs and on-device maths solver are free with no account. AI help needs a free sign-in and includes a number of answers per day; Chalkmind Plus (with a free trial) gives you many more.

Do I need a stylus?

No, a finger works. A stylus feels more like paper and makes neater handwriting, which helps Chalkmind read it.

Which subjects does it help with?

Maths and science problems most directly, including algebra, geometry, physics and chemistry. The assistant can also explain, summarise and quiz you on any subject.

How old do I need to be?

Chalkmind is designed for students aged 13 and up. Younger students should use it with a parent or teacher.

Can I use it on a phone?

Yes, it works on Android phones, though a tablet gives you much more room to write.
