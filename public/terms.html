Terms of use

Last updated: 15 November 2026

By using Chalkmind you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

Using Chalkmind

  • Use the app for learning and teaching, and only with content you have the right to use (for example PDFs you are allowed to copy).
  • Don't use AI features to create harmful, abusive or illegal content, or to try to break or overload the service.

Your account

AI features need a free account, created when you sign in with Google. Keep access to your Google account secure. We may suspend accounts that break these terms. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Account → Delete account).

AI answers

AI answers can be wrong. Check important results, and use the steps and explanations to understand the work rather than to replace it. You can report a problem answer with the flag button next to it, or email support@chalkmind.tech.

Free plan and Chalkmind Plus

  • The free plan includes a daily number of AI requests. Chalkmind Plus raises the daily limit, subject to fair use. Limits reset every day at 00:00 UTC.
  • Chalkmind Plus is a subscription sold through Google Play, billed monthly or yearly at the price shown in the app. It renews automatically until you cancel it in Google Play.
  • If a free trial is offered, you are charged when it ends unless you cancel before then. Each Google account can use the trial once.
  • You can cancel at any time in Google Play. Plus stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

Your content

Your boards and files belong to you. They are stored on your device; keep your own backups by exporting boards you care about.

Availability

We work to keep Chalkmind reliable, but the app and its AI features are provided "as is", without guarantees, and features may change. AI features have usage limits and may be paused for maintenance.
