Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Chalkmind is a whiteboard app for teachers and students. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. If you have a question, email support@chalkmind.tech.

The short version

  • Your boards, notes and imported PDFs are stored on your device. We never upload them.
  • Drawing, shapes, offline maths, PDF import and export work without an account.
  • AI features need a free account (Sign in with Google). When you use one, only what is needed for that request is sent, and we don't keep it.
  • There are no ads, no advertising trackers and no sale of data.

Data stored on your device

Boards (your writing, shapes, text and graphs), PDFs you import, and app settings are saved in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Chalkmind does not upload them to us. Your sign-in session is kept in the device's secure storage.

Your Chalkmind account

When you sign in with Google, Google tells us your account ID, email address, name and profile picture. We store these to identify your account, along with:

  • when you joined and when you last used the app;
  • how many AI requests you made each day (to apply the daily limit of your plan), and for each request the time, the feature used, whether it succeeded and how long it took. These request records are deleted after 30 days;
  • your Chalkmind Plus subscription status, if you have one (see below);
  • answers you report and feedback you send, including the email address you give us.

We use this data only to run the service: signing you in, applying plan limits, preventing abuse, answering support requests and fixing problems. Account data is stored on our servers and with our database hosting provider.

AI features

Some features use an AI model: solving a problem the device can't solve on its own, the AI Assistant (explain, hint, check my work, quizzes and similar), diagram generation, and looking up highlighted text in a PDF. When you use one, the app sends the content needed for that request to our server:

  • an image of the part of the board you selected or are working on, or of the highlighted area of a PDF page;
  • typed text from the board or PDF that is relevant to the request, and the question or topic you typed;
  • a photo you choose to attach;
  • for follow-up questions, the last few messages of that conversation.

Our server forwards the request to our AI provider, OpenRouter, which passes it to the AI model that produces the answer (for example a Google Gemini model). Their handling of request data is governed by their own policies. We don't store your questions, board images or the AI's answers, except an answer you choose to report (with the question it answered), which we keep to review it. Please don't include personal information about yourself or others in content you send to the AI.

Subscriptions

Chalkmind Plus is sold through Google Play. Google handles payment; we never see your card or payment details. Google Play tells us the subscription's status (for example active, in free trial, cancelled or expired), its plan, renewal and expiry dates, order ID and country, which we store to unlock Plus for your account.

What we don't do

  • No advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs in the app.
  • We don't sell or share personal data for marketing.
  • We don't use your content to train AI models.

Permissions

Chalkmind uses internet access for sign-in, AI features and subscriptions. It opens files (PDFs, images) only when you choose them in the system file picker, and only reads the files you pick.

Children and schools

Chalkmind is designed for learning and can be used by students. We recommend that younger students use AI features with a teacher or parent, and that schools review this policy before use.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Account → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, usage history, reports and feedback from our servers. Subscription records (status and order ID) are kept without any link to you, as required for accounting. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription: cancel it in Google Play first.

If you can't use the app, email support@chalkmind.tech from the address you signed in with and we will delete the account within 30 days.

Your choices

  • Use the whiteboard without signing in. AI features are then off.
  • Sign out at any time in Account. Your boards stay on your device.
  • Delete boards in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all local data.
  • Contact us at support@chalkmind.tech to access or correct your data, or with any privacy request.

Changes

If we change how data is handled, we will update this page before the change is released and change the date above.
